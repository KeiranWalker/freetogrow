Level 3

## Models Used

## Description

Initial AI usage was at a level 2, with prompts mainly helping with syntax of flutter (an unfamiliar language to most team members).

Towards the end, AI was used to help debug larger issues that we would not have had time to do manually, pushing us up to a level 3.
